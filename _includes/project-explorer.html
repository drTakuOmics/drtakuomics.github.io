Published · Science Advances · 2025

Opening the gate to protein clearance

In C. elegans, 20S gate opening enhances disordered-protein clearance and resistance to proteotoxic stress.

The question

Can constitutive 20S gate opening improve protein quality control in a living organism?

The approach

An α3ΔN open-gate model connects biochemical degradation assays with RNA-seq, quantitative proteomics, and organismal stress phenotypes.

Why it matters

Gate regulation can reshape proteostasis. The work supports a mechanistic strategy for proteinopathies; it does not establish a treatment in humans.

C. elegans · biochemistry · RNA-seq + proteomics

Published · BMC Genomics · 2024

A short motif. A broad discovery space.

ProEnd makes HbYX-containing proteins searchable across organisms, connecting sequence discovery with biochemical validation.

The question

Which proteins across the tree of life carry a C-terminal HbYX motif?

The approach

The published survey screened approximately 73 million proteins from 22,000 reference proteomes and assembled an accessible database.

Why it matters

An HbYX match nominates a candidate. Binding and activation require experimental evidence; sequence occurrence alone does not establish either.

Comparative biology · sequence analysis · database development

Published · PLOS One · 2026

Protein folding meets neuronal metabolism

The study identifies 226 candidate TRiC-associated proteins and links impaired folding to disrupted photoreceptor energy metabolism.

The question

How does the TRiC/CCT chaperonin support rod photoreceptor function?

The approach

Affinity purification proteomics, perturbation models, and integrated proteomic–metabolomic analysis map TRiC function in mouse rods.

Why it matters

Folding capacity connects structural integrity, signaling, and bioenergetics. The proposed Rab10–GLUT4 connection remains a mechanistic hypothesis.

Mouse photoreceptors · proteomics + metabolomics · co-first author

The question

The approach

Adipose tissues collected during cesarean delivery were profiled in gestational-diabetes patients and matched controls.

Published · BMC Genomics · 2020

Adipose adaptation around calving

Postpartum transcriptomes connect lipid mobilization with inflammation, tissue remodeling, and redox signaling.

The question

How does adipose gene expression change during the transition to lactation?

The approach

Subcutaneous adipose tissue from 12 Holstein cows was sampled before calving and at two postpartum time points, then analyzed by RNA-seq.

Why it matters

The longitudinal design resolves coordinated tissue adaptation. Enrichment and regulator predictions nominate mechanisms for further testing.

Longitudinal RNA-seq · functional enrichment · co-first author

Published · Alzheimer Dis. Assoc. Disord. · 2019

Epigenetic variation in a Colombian cohort

Lower methylation at three measured CpGs was associated with Alzheimer disease in this Colombian case–control cohort.

The question

Is peripheral-blood BIN1 methylation associated with late-onset Alzheimer disease?

The approach

Eight CpGs were measured in 50 cases and 50 matched controls; regression models accounted for age, sex, and APOE.

Why it matters

This is an association in peripheral blood. Causality, brain relevance, and clinical biomarker performance require independent validation.

Human epigenetics · case–control analysis · master’s research
