## Adipose transcriptomes in gestational diabetes

**Research question:** How do adipose-tissue transcriptomes vary with gestational diabetes and anatomical location?

**Approach:** Omental and subcutaneous adipose tissue collected during cesarean delivery was profiled by RNA-seq in patients with gestational diabetes and matched controls.

**Contribution:** This published dataset provides whole-transcriptome profiles for exploring differences associated with disease status and adipose depot. It supports secondary analyses of gene expression and biological pathways.

**Interpretation:** This is a data resource for hypothesis generation; it does not establish a validated diagnostic signature or therapeutic target.
